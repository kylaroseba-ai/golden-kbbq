These shape everything else. My recommendation is the first option in each.

  1. Two Supabase projects, not branches. Keep your existing project Golden Korean BBQ (Singapore, already has all 8 migrations, empty) as PROD. Create a second free project Golden Korean BBQ – Test in Singapore as TEST. Supabase branching needs a paid plan and costs about US$0.013 per hour per branch; a second free project costs nothing.

  4. Nobody can sign themselves up. Public sign-up is turned off in Supabase. Owner, manager and admin logins are invited by email and set their own password. Shared tablets and phones are registered by a manager from inside the app. Staff without email get a PIN only.

  6. Upgrade PROD to Supabase Pro before opening day. Free projects pause after a week with no activity and have no downloadable daily backups, which the spec calls unacceptable for a POS. Building and testing can stay on Free.

4. Accounts: three kinds of sign-in

The same three kinds the app uses today, now backed by real Supabase Auth. I never type or store anyone's password: invited people set their own, and device secrets are generated by the server.

Personal login

Owner, manager, admin. Email + password, on their own phone or laptop.

How it's made: the owner (or admin) enters a name, email and role in Setup → People. The server sends a Supabase invite email; the person sets a password and lands in the app.

First one: JD's owner invite is sent once by a setup script you run, or from the Supabase dashboard.

Shared device

Front counter, each server phone, the kitchen tablet. Signs in once and stays signed in until revoked.

How it's made: a manager signs in on the new tablet, opens Setup → Devices → "Register this device", names it and picks its kind. The server creates the device's own login with a random secret and hands it to that tablet only.

Lost phone: the manager taps Revoke; every session on it stops working.

Staff PIN

Cashiers, servers, kitchen staff. No email needed. They tap their name on a shared device and enter a 4–6 digit PIN.

How it's made: the manager adds them in Setup → Staff with role and language, and sets a starting PIN. Five wrong PINs lock that person on that device for 15 minutes.

Leaving staff: set inactive; their history stays.

5. Data: 57 tables in six areas

All tables exist in PROD today. Every row carries a branch_id, and every change goes through a database function that checks the role and writes the audit log. Additions planned in this phase are marked NEW.

Accounts & access

10 tables
  • organizations, branches: the business, each restaurant, its BIR header and settings
  • profiles: every person; email NEW, PIN (server-only), language, active
  • user_branch_roles: one role per person per branch
  • devices, pos_terminals: registered tablets/phones; the BIR cashier terminal
  • staff_tokens, pin_attempts: PIN sign-ins and lockouts
  • idempotency_keys, audit_log: offline retries applied once; who changed what

Inventory

18 tables
  • item_categories, items, item_stock_levels: what we stock, alert and par levels, live on-hand and average cost
  • stock_batches, stock_movements: FIFO batches with expiry; the ledger, never edited
  • suppliers, supplier_items, deliveries, delivery_lines
  • prep_recipes, prep_recipe_inputs, production_runs, production_run_inputs
  • waste_logs, stock_counts, stock_count_lines, reorder_lists, reorder_lines

Menu, tables & POS

14 tables
  • dining_tables: floor layout and status (free, seated, billing, dirty)
  • menu_categories, menu_items, menu_item_price_history, discount_types
  • package_refill_items, portion_rules: which cuts a package allows; grams each sale deducts
  • table_sessions, session_pax, order_lines: one seating, its guests, what was ordered
  • refill_rounds, refill_lines, kitchen_tickets, kitchen_ticket_lines

Billing & BIR

7 tables
  • bills, payments, sc_pwd_records: totals, split payments, SC/PWD names and IDs (restricted)
  • invoices: gap-free numbers per terminal or manual booklet number; void, never delete
  • cash_shifts, x_readings, z_readings: cashier float, X per shift, Z per day with grand total

Staff & shifts

2 tables
  • shift_schedules: who works when, position, break minutes
  • time_entries: time in, break start/end, time out, minutes late, manager corrections

Reservations & messages

6 tables
  • reservations, reservation_slots_config: bookings and capacity per slot
  • message_templates, outbound_messages: SMS/email text and the send queue
  • notifications, push_subscriptions: staff alerts, phone push
